ICP definition.

Companies running production workloads on AWS, Azure, or OpenShift. 200–5,000 employees. Engineering-led buying motion. Primary pain: incident resolution time, MTTR, and on-call burden. Industries: FinServ, SaaS, infrastructure, and cloud-native.

Scoring criteria.

Firmographic fit (industry, size, stack) + engagement signal (event attendance, email engagement, site behavior) + timing signal (budget cycle, initiative trigger). Formal scoring model to be configured in CRM by end of July. Until then: SDR judgment + RevOps spot-check.

Disqualification criteria.

No production cloud workloads. Fewer than 50 engineers. Competitor employee. Timeline beyond 12 months with no urgency driver. SDRs should recycle, not delete — timing changes. Recycled leads re-enter nurture sequence.

Attribution model.

First-touch and last-touch attribution tracked. Marketing credited for first-touch source (event, inbound, content). SDR credited for SQL creation. AE credited for closed-won. Multi-touch attribution report runs monthly — RevOps owns.

Handoff Protocol.
The rules of engagement at every transition point. Who owns the lead, what must be documented before it moves, and how both sides confirm the handoff is complete.
MQL → SDR Handoff

Marketing hands off an MQL to the SDR queue when the lead hits the MQL threshold. SDR accepts ownership by logging a first activity within the SLA window. An MQL with no SDR activity within 4 hours is escalated to the SDR manager.

Checkpoint Owner Required Action SLA
MQL threshold hit Marketing / CRM automation Lead status updated to MQL. SDR queue notified via CRM alert and Slack #sdr-queue Automated, instant
SDR first touch SDR (assigned by round-robin) Log first activity in CRM. Initial outreach sent (email or call). Add to active sequence 4 hrs from MQL status
SDR qualification SDR Discovery questions asked and documented in CRM contact notes. ICP confirmed or disqualified Within 5 business days of first touch
MQL → SQL conversion SDR Status updated to SQL. Required fields populated (pain, EB, timeline). Discovery call completed Before AE intro scheduled
SDR → AE Handoff

An SQL becomes an AE-owned opportunity when the discovery call is complete and the required MEDDPIC fields are populated. The SDR sends a warm intro email — no cold transfers. AE must accept the opportunity within 24 hours.

Required Field What to Document Where

Economic Buyer Name and title of the person who controls the budget. Do they know NeuBird AI exists? CRM Opportunity — Economic Buyer field

AE → CS Handoff (Closed-Won)

When a deal closes, the AE initiates a CS handoff within 48 hours. The handoff document is created in Notion (template linked in CRM) and the CS intro call is scheduled before the AE exits the relationship.

Handoff document.

AE completes the CS handoff doc: company context, key contacts, pain statement, what was sold, success criteria agreed in the deal, and any commitments made. No verbal handoffs — everything in writing before the intro call.

Intro call.

AE, CS, and champion on a 30-minute intro call. AE introduces CS, recaps the pain and what was bought, confirms success criteria, and then steps back. CS owns the relationship from day one of the call.

What a good handoff looks like.

Champion knows CS's name before the intro call. Every required CRM field is populated. Success criteria are written down and agreed by the customer. CS can run the relationship without calling the AE for context in week one.

What a bad handoff looks like.

AE sends a single Slack message. CS discovers commitments that weren't in the contract. The champion gets a surprise email from someone they've never heard of. RevOps reviews handoff quality monthly — AEs are accountable.

MEDDPIC Rollout.
NeuBird AI's qualification and deal inspection framework for H2 2026. MEDDPIC fields live in the CRM, inspection happens in the weekly deal review, and adoption is tracked by RevOps.
The Framework

MEDDPIC is a qualification framework that ensures every deal in the pipeline has the information needed to forecast accurately and close. At NeuBird AI, MEDDPIC is required on all opportunities at Stage 2 and above.

M
Metrics
What is the quantified business impact? Time saved, incidents resolved, MTTR reduced. The prospect's number, not ours.
At NeuBird AI: "How many engineer-hours per week does your team spend on incident response? What would a 50% reduction mean for your roadmap?"
E
Economic Buyer
Who controls the budget? Have we met them? Do they see the value? The champion is not always the EB.
At NeuBird AI: typically VP Engineering or CTO. FinServ deals may involve CIO. Must be named in CRM before Stage 2.
D
Decision Criteria
What does the prospect use to evaluate vendors? Technical criteria, security requirements, integration needs, pricing model.
At NeuBird AI: AWS-native integration, RBAC + audit trail, MTTR reduction benchmark, trial results. Document what they weight most heavily.
D
Decision Process
How do they make the decision? Who is involved, in what order, and what are the approval steps and legal/procurement gates?
At NeuBird AI: map the process before Stage 3. Procurement timelines often slip. Know the legal review cycle early and factor into close date.
P
Identify Pain
What is the specific, named pain? Is it felt by the EB? Is there urgency? Pain without urgency doesn't close.
At NeuBird AI: "on-call fatigue", "incident RCA takes 4 hours", "same incidents keep recurring". The champion must feel it personally.
C
Champion
Who inside the account is selling for us when we're not in the room? Do they have power, credibility, and access to the EB?
At NeuBird AI: usually a senior SRE or Platform Engineering lead. Test the champion: "Can you get us 30 minutes with your VP?" If no, they're a coach, not a champion.

Deal Review Framework
Question What We're Testing Red Flag
"What's the business impact if they don't solve this?" Is the pain real and quantified? Is there urgency? AE says "they want to improve efficiency" — no number, no urgency
"Who controls the budget and have you met them?" Is the EB identified and engaged? AE hasn't met the EB. Champion says "I'll handle it"
"What does winning look like for your champion?" Does the champion have personal skin in the game? Champion's success isn't tied to solving the problem
"What happens between now and close?" Is the decision process mapped? Are there hidden gates? AE says "we just need to do a POC" — legal, security, procurement not scoped
"Why would they choose someone else?" Is competition identified and a response prepared? AE says "there's no real competition" — almost never true

Source of truth.

The CRM is the single source of truth for pipeline and revenue data. If it's not in the CRM, it doesn't exist for reporting purposes. RevOps enforces data hygiene — missing fields are flagged weekly, not quarterly.

Reporting outputs.

Weekly: Slack digest in #revenue-ops by 9am Monday. Monthly: shared Google Doc report to Marketing, Sales, and Leadership. Quarterly: slides in the QBR deck. Dashboards: TBD pending CRM configuration.
