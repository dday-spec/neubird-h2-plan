The Motion.
One coordinated motion across three plays. The Founding SDR runs all three, learns what lands with engineering buyers, and turns what works into a playbook the next reps inherit.
Inbound · Outbound · Allbound

First on the ground: qualifying inbound demand the moment a buyer raises a hand, running outbound into target accounts, and orchestrating the allbound plays that tie campaigns, intent signals, and prospecting into one coordinated motion.

Play 01
Inbound
Speed-to-lead
Qualify and route hand-raisers from the website, content, and events before they go cold. Win on speed — respond fast the moment a buyer raises a hand.
Play 02
Outbound
Build & test
Build and test outbound sequences across email, phone, and LinkedIn into target accounts, then refine based on what converts. Fresh eyes on the motion, not a handed-down script.
Play 03
Allbound
Orchestrate
Turn marketing campaigns, intent signals, and account activity into timely, coordinated outreach — the motion that ties inbound and outbound together so they feed each other.

The Angle

The pain they feel.

Engineering teams lose roughly 40% of their time to incidents — pulled off the roadmap to firefight problems that page them at the worst possible hour. They're drowning in production noise and piecing every incident story together across five tools.

What we offer them.

The Production Ops Agent catches issues before they page anyone, resolves incidents without an engineer reconstructing the story, and turns every investigation into operating knowledge. Production stays running, so engineers don't have to.

Why they reply.

Real proof points they can feel: up to 90% MTTR reduction and 94% RCA accuracy across 120,000+ production investigations. The job is to make a busy engineering leader stop scrolling — strong, specific written outreach that speaks to their reality.

How we learn.

Figure out what actually lands with SRE leads and engineering managers, then turn what works into a playbook. Partner with marketing and product to refine the message based on real buyer reactions, not assumptions.
